Methodology

The index estimates public sanctions-expansion pressure over the next 0–30 days. It does not estimate whether any person or company will be designated. Every input is public, timestamped, and fetched without a paid account.

35%

OFAC action velocity

Designation-linked releases in the latest seven days, compared with 12 prior non-overlapping weeks using median and MAD.

20%

SDN delta breadth

Exact added and removed IDs between consecutive accepted official SDN CSV snapshots, with program and entity-type breadth.

25%

Official posture shift

Predeclared coercive and relief terms in official Treasury/OFAC titles, normalized against the same weekly baseline.

20%

Energy dislocation

Five-session Brent, WTI, and Brent–WTI spread moves compared with robust historical baselines from FRED.

Aggregation. Available components are reweighted to sum to 100%. A five-point concurrence bonus applies only when at least one official indicator and the independent market indicator are both elevated. This limits correlated double-counting. Missing sources never become zero; cached data expires after 72 hours and failed refreshes preserve the last-good publication.
Reproduction. GitHub Actions runs the pipeline every six hours, executes tests, stores source snapshots and calculated output in the repository, then deploys the same data shown here. Formulae, terms, windows, evidence and source URLs are emitted in data/output.json.
